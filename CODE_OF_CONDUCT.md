# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
